# Formation Move 2.0

**Developer:** reforged25-source / Codex  
**Version:** 2.0  
**License:** GNU GPL, v2 or later  

Tactical military formation movement (Box, Circle, Line, Wedge, Column) with coherent lateral projection matching and zero-GC batched rendering.

### Controls:
- Middle Click or Middle + Right Click drag to position, shape, and aim unit formations.
- Options configurable in Settings > Custom > Formation Move 2.0.
